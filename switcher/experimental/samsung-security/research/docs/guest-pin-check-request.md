selected-user record and ID. Readback checks that the controller is resumed,

The source can retire the exact occurrence from another thread. Adoption,
start reservation, and the final ticket claim order their commit with that
retirement. External reads stay outside the locks. A reentrant nested claim
refuses while the outer claim observes the source. An observation failure

values. The main-thread settled continuation releases its own transition
once. Settling a stale or unrelated transition leaves other reservations
