# OneOS compatibility

What testers reported from the OneOS app (**Send to the OneOS team** on the Last session screen), by OneOS version, phone and system. Each cell is how many reports said it works, of the reports that tried it (✅ all, ⚠️ some, ❌ none, – nobody tried it yet). **Started** counts the reports that reached the home screen. Only the answers are counted here: no names, notes or phone details.

Updated 2026-10-29. Every system is still experimental: read the warnings in the [README](../README.md) first.
