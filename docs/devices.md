# Which phones OneOS runs on

Every phone the OneOS app supports, and for each system what testers reported in the last days (✅ started every time, ⚠️ started on some tries, ❌ did not start, then the checklist items most of them marked as a problem), what the team tested (✅ tested by the team), 🔲 not tested yet, or 🔜 comes with the next update; 🕓 phones that sent findings but need One UI 8 or newer first, or are tablets. Generated by `reports/devices.py` from the app's phone list and the reports sent from the app (**Send to the OneOS team**): counts only.

Updated 2026-11-11. Every system is still experimental: read the warnings in the [README](../README.md) first.
